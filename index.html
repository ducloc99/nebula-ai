How it works

Three steps, no config files to write.

STEP 01

Point it at a repo

One command connects Nebula to your Git provider. It clones, indexes, and builds a dependency graph of the entire codebase in the background.

STEP 02

Ask it anything, in plain language

From the CLI, your IDE, or Slack — ask why something works the way it does, and get an answer that cites the actual file and commit.

STEP 03

Keep it in sync automatically

Every merge re-indexes only what changed. Nebula's understanding of your code is never more than a few minutes stale.
